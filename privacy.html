Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Pilot free tools including Reply, Business Writer, Resume Helper, and Message Check

This Privacy Policy explains how Pilot (operated by DINESHGOPISUNKARA) handles information when you use our website and free AI tools (including Reply, Business Writer, Resume Helper, and Message Check). Contact: dineshgopisunkara0228@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Pilot is a free AI tools platform. Public founder credit: DINESHGOPISUNKARA.

2. What we collect

  • Feedback form. If you submit the on-site feedback form, we receive the fields you type: usefulness, next-tool suggestion, optional email, and comments. A honeypot field reduces spam.
  • Tool inputs (local). Messages, notes, context, tone, language, and drafts are processed in your browser for the client-side composers. Message Check also scores pasted text locally. We do not receive those contents unless you paste them into feedback or email us.
  • Usage counters (local). Each tool stores a daily generation count in your browser’s localStorage to enforce a fair daily limit: pilot_reply_usage_v2, pilot_writer_usage_v1, pilot_resume_usage_v1, and pilot_check_usage_v1. Those counters stay on your device.
  • Technical logs. Our host (Netlify) may process standard server logs (IP, user agent, referrer, timestamps) for security and reliability.
  • PWA cache (local). If you install Pilot or use the service worker, static assets may be cached on your device for offline shell performance. Cached files stay on your device.

3. Payments

This site does not collect payment card details. There is no checkout on this free-tools hub.

4. How we use information

Feedback helps improve Pilot. Host logs support security and reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We use Netlify to host the site and receive form submissions. They process data under their own terms as a service provider.

6. Retention

Form submissions are retained as needed to review feedback. Local storage and caches remain until you clear them in your browser.

7. Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser, stop using the tools, or email us to request deletion of feedback you submitted with an email address.

8. Children

Pilot is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
